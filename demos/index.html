Porch pickup

Every order gets its own cabinet code.

  • The customer picks a pickup day and time at checkout.
  • Their code works for 24 hours from then, and only once.
  • The receipt shows the code, the address and a map link.

The porch cabinet

A QR sticker on the cabinet.

  • Customers who ordered ahead can look up their code.
  • Drive-by visitors buy what's inside and get a code on the spot.
  • The hub tracks what's left in the cabinet and every time it opens.
The lock: igloohome Smart Padlock 2

A keypad padlock for the cabinet's latch. It's waterproof, runs on a rechargeable battery and needs no Wi-Fi. The site creates each order's code through igloohome's API. How it works
