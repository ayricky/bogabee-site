Our Story

About Bogabee.

A small-batch apiary rooted in Manalapan Township, New Jersey.

Est. 2022

The Beginning

It Started With a Single Hive.

What began as a backyard fascination with honeybees quickly turned into a passion. Bogabee Apiaries was born from a deep respect for these incredible pollinators and the desire to share the purest honey possible with our community.

Based in Manalapan Township, NJ, we manage our hives with care and intention. Every jar of Bogabee honey is raw, unfiltered, and completely natural — the way honey was meant to be enjoyed.

We believe in keeping things small, keeping things local, and keeping things honest. No additives, no shortcuts, no compromise.

What We Stand For

Our Values.

100% Raw & Pure

Never heated, never filtered, never blended. Our honey goes straight from the hive to the jar, preserving all the natural enzymes, pollen, and flavor.

Locally Rooted

Our bees forage on New Jersey wildflowers, giving each harvest a unique, seasonal character. Every jar is a taste of the Garden State.

Made With Heart

Every jar is hand-poured, every label placed with care. This isn't a factory operation — it's a labor of love from our family to yours.

The Apiary

Happy Bees, Better Honey.

We practice sustainable beekeeping that puts the health of our colonies first. Our bees are never treated with harsh chemicals, and we always leave them enough honey to thrive through the winter.

The result? Honey that's not only delicious but produced ethically. When you buy from Bogabee, you're supporting bees, supporting local agriculture, and supporting a small family business.
